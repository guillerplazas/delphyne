## Mechanism findings and the guard limitation

A correctly abstains on the nonconvertible real-power `change` state where
F attempts a repair. But A also falsely claims nonconvertibility on the
Rocq-checked `Rsqr`/multiplication state of amc12a_2002_p13. F repairs that
state and permits `exact H.` to discharge the obligation. This is the only
registered useful-transition difference between A and F: the selected
negative example helps one abstention but does not establish reliable
state-specific conversion judgment.

All three typed arms still propose an unavailable `sq_nonneg` application
on mathd_algebra_323. The local checker rejects it; the example bank does
not reliably teach the missing-reference abstention. Plain `nra` also fails
to bridge the square normalization state. General syntax matching and a
shared real-arithmetic vocabulary do not resolve these applicability gaps.

The finite safety grammar was too restrictive for one positive state:
Q, F and A use separate `pose proof ... .` and `nra.` sentences (A also
names the introduced hypothesis), while the registered guard admits the
semicolon form. **All three proposed suffixes execute in their actual
state under an independent live Rocq check.** They were rejected before
kernel execution by the registered guard, not by Rocq and not by an
experiment-platform failure. The limitation concerns admission coverage;
there was no unsound accepted proof.

`guard_audit.json` records eleven post-run local checks, with zero API calls.
`guard_sensitivity.json` adds those three verified suffixes only as a clearly
post-hoc sensitivity: Q/F/A useful transitions become 3/4/3 and positive
counts 5/6/5. A still loses a useful transition to F, has only 4/6 negative
abstentions, and fails expansion. The primary report, artifacts, source
freeze and measurements are unchanged. No relaxed guard was deployed or
used for new paid work.

The set/alias on mathd_algebra_185 and the large-natural representation
change on amc12a_2016_p2 execute in both F/A, but do not count as useful
progress by themselves. This stronger distinction prevents prefix growth
or a syntactically accepted assertion from selecting a recovery mechanism.

## Interpretation and disposition

This is a bounded negative result for the **registered adaptive delivery
mechanism**, with an explicit admission-coverage limitation. It does not
show that verified examples never help: F has one additional useful local
transition over Q, with insufficient evidence of transfer or deployment
savings. Nor does it establish superiority of automated learning: the bank
content was held fixed and no reflection/teacher episode was bought.

Do not broaden the playbook, increase reasoning or buy recovery attempts
from this result. Existing hint #122 now records the transfer and guard
limitations. If pursued later, the concrete question is whether conversion
and reference support can be established using existing local Rocq tools,
with any gain attributed to that tool/control change. A future guard change
would need separate offline semantic checks and a new frozen campaign;
this result grants no additional spending authorization.
