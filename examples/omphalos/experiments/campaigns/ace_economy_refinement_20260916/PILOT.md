Both independent ACE refinements are promising enough to benchmark in
combination: each adds one proof; compact also costs less. Non-ACE reset
adds cost without another proof in this small pilot. The final factorial
therefore retains its baseline and compact-only alternatives; non-ACE is
never forced to reset for the headline comparison. No statistical threshold
or per-replicate solve requirement is being used as an acceptance gate.

Implementation checks found no required correction. Nine resets were
followed by paid requests; every one cleared opaque reasoning to the initial
empty state. The window only removes complete history groups and adds no
handoff, summary, retrieval memory, failure notes or tool. The compact
system is 3518 versus 8577 characters for non-ACE, and 12216 versus 17275
for ACE, including its unchanged playbook.

All 16 unsuccessful cells ended at monetary admission for the next request,
with the existing conservative reservation intact. Their terminal feedback
shows proof/focus/syntax/name errors; a bounded Rocq timeout remained
explicitly unavailable evidence. An unsafe proposal was rejected. These
are observed model/prover failures, not accepted invalid proofs or reasons
to add problem-specific mathematical guidance.

The treatment sources remain frozen. No correction reruns were used.
