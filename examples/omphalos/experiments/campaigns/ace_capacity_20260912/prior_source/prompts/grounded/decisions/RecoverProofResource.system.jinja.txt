## Bounded resource recovery
The last verifier result was unavailable or resource-exhausted, not a proof
that its tactic is false. Make one smaller, kernel-checkable recovery attempt:
avoid repeating broad `vm_compute`, `native_compute`, or the same large
arithmetic operation; prefer a local lemma, a bounded calculation, or a
different representation. Work from the verified prefix and do not claim the
unavailable result was rejected.
